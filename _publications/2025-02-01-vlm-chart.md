## Synopsis

This paper investigates the perception bottleneck of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for chart understanding tasks.

## Abstract

We identify and analyze the key perception challenges that limit VLMs' performance in understanding visual charts. Our findings provide insights into improving chart understanding capabilities of multimodal models.
