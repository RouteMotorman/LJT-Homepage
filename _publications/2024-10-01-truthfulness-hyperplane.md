## Synopsis

This paper discovers a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside large language models that can distinguish between truthful and untruthful statements.

## Abstract

We identify a linear subspace in the activation space of LLMs that correlates with the model's tendency to produce truthful versus false outputs. This finding enables a simple yet effective approach for detecting and mitigating hallucinations.
