## Synopsis

This paper proposes using in-context sharpness as an alert mechanism for detecting and mitigating hallucinations in LLMs from an inner representation perspective.

## Abstract

We introduce a novel metric based on the sharpness of in-context representations that can effectively identify when a model is likely to produce hallucinated content, enabling targeted mitigation strategies.
