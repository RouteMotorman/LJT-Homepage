## Synopsis

C-Eval is a comprehensive multi-level multi-discipline evaluation suite designed for assessing the capabilities of foundation models in Chinese language understanding and reasoning.

## Abstract

We present C-Eval, a large-scale evaluation benchmark consisting of 13,457 multiple-choice questions across 52 subjects covering humanities, social sciences, STEM, and more. This benchmark provides a comprehensive assessment of foundation models' knowledge and reasoning abilities in Chinese.
