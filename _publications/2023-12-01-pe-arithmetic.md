## Synopsis

This paper explores composing parameter-efficient modules (PEMs) using arithmetic operations to create new model capabilities without additional training.

## Abstract

We demonstrate that parameter-efficient modules can be composed through simple arithmetic operations, enabling the creation of new model behaviors and capabilities by combining existing modules in novel ways.
